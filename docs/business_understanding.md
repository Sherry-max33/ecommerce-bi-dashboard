# Business Understanding

## Context

Brazilian e-commerce marketplace (Olist dataset): orders, customers, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and geolocation.

## Stakeholders

- **Operations**: delivery times, order status, regional performance
- **Marketing**: category trends, customer satisfaction
- **Finance**: revenue, payment mix, freight costs

## Questions to answer

1. What are revenue and order trends over time?
2. Which categories and regions drive growth?
3. How do delivery delays and review scores affect repeat behavior?
4. What is the near-term revenue forecast?

## Success criteria

- Reproducible pipeline from raw CSV → PostgreSQL → dashboards
- Documented KPIs aligned with `docs/kpi_definitions.md`
